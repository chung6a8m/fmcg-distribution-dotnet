# APP-02C.4 Partial Delivery and Due-Dated AR

- **R02.** A Delivery consumes the Order's frozen Confirm ShipTo or a newer immutable amendment. A current Sales checker with `case_checker` approval can approve a fresh C.1 selection made by another actor. Approval requires a reason and the observed version. It applies to the Order's undelivered lines at approval and preserves all earlier sources. Reject reused or superseded selections, stale versions and fully delivered orders. The posting command compares the observed version to the current version inside the transaction.
- **R03.** Verify immutable C.2 policy lineage, canonical hash, and approval effective at Confirm. Use the recorded terms, unit price, discount, tax and approved seller timezone. Calculate the UTC receipt's seller-local calendar date, then add N calendar days. Current role and writer authority still apply. The amount uses cumulative four-place rounding across deliveries of the same Order line so split postings reconcile with the frozen gross on completion.
- **R04.** A posting names a C.3 loaded manifest line and actual delivered quantity. Validate its exact reservation, cumulative issues and remaining order/right/manifest quantity. Consume the right once, issue TRIP stock and cost, and write source-linked AR and COGS together. The rest stays held. If only part of the Order was initially reserved, Reserve can cover the still-unreserved amount after subtracting actual deliveries. Later delivery or separately approved release uses its own source. The synthetic occurred-delivery receipt claims the common command ID before posting. An uncertain posting leaves that receipt visible for review and blocks a new command on the same loaded line. An exact retry preserves the original receipt time and payload, including after the browser closes.

For DEV, reserve and load 40 EA from MAIN at carrying cost 10 per EA. Post 25 EA at frozen unit price 15 and N=7. Expect AR 375, COGS 250, 15 EA held in TRIP, physical MAIN 60 and TRIP 15, and due date equal to the recorded seller date plus seven. Confirm and Load do not post AR. Check authority, amendments, stale snapshots, overdelivery, same-ID recovery, concurrent submissions and rollback. Reuse the existing React controls and nonce-owned LocalDB launcher. Physical acceptance and C.5 reporting remain separate.

Run `./scripts/Run-App02C4.ps1 -Verify` from the repository root. The interactive launcher opens the synthetic Delivery and AR workspace at `/sales/delivery`. When an Order has an unresolved occurred-delivery receipt, approval of a ShipTo amendment requires reconciliation first. The DEV UI supports exact posting retry for a pending receipt owned by the current writer. Disposition of rejected physical evidence and approval of release remain separate audited business actions.

## Source architecture

- **Independent audit SQL Control Plane:** `src/FmcgDms.Preprod.Operations/Migrations/20261118_001_immutable_audit.sql`, version `B8_20261118_001`, is DBA-applied only and distinct from tenant databases. Schema replay fails if unknown version or required guard/trigger/procedure/role was removed, or an immutable trigger was disabled. Nothing runs at API/Worker startup, grants business access, chooses a tenant from HTTP, creates an admin, seeds retention or activates business. SQL `b8_writer` and `b8_hold` roles only execute their corresponding stored procedures after separate DBA-granted login and `B8WriterScope` mapping; `SUSER_SNAME()` binds the real SQL login, not client-supplied actor.
- **Append-only ledger:** `b8_Append` obtains a tenant-specific SQL transaction lock, creates sequential immutable events and updates a last-hash head atomically. Payload is a SHA256 of previously redacted evidence; lineage includes TenantId, EntryId, Kind, SQL principal hash, CorrelationId, SourceId, PolicyVersionId, MessageId, previous hash and sequence. `B8AuditVerifier` independently rehashes SQL records, detects missing/reordered/modified records, and validates signed ECDSA P-256 external checkpoints. Update/delete triggers and no table DML grants protect ordinary users; **an elevated DBA can remove them**, so immutable custody requires a separate cryptographic off-host anchored key/service with independently fetched receipts. CI signs with an ephemeral key in the same test runner, **not** real off-host storage.
- **Telemetry and operations:** `B8Telemetry` exposes .NET ActivitySource and Meter, strongly typed GUID correlation and no arbitrary secret-bearing attributes, but **no OTel collector/exporter or API/Worker/SQL/inbox/outbox wiring**. `B8AlertVerifier` requires rule version, independent destination delivery and ACK, deadline and escalation; `B8IncidentVerifier` validates incident owner, handoff, severity authority, runbook, escalation and independent reviewed closeout. These are **pure witness validators**, not a real alert/pager/incident backend.
- **Retention and holds:** SQL `B8LegalHold` append-only, with separate hold role and no ReleaseHold/DELETE/purge endpoint. `B8RetentionGate` is a non-destructive dry-run requiring separate Maker, Checker, reviewer, legal authority and class-specific retention approval, legal hold absence, full immutable archive and independent manifest/peer evidence. Only explicitly eligible *diagnostic telemetry* may be a candidate for later purge; durable APP-02A receipt/source/CASE/inbox/outbox, APP-01 business source/ledger, audit/security/recovery/legal evidence cannot be automatically TTL-purged. Legal retention durations and governing jurisdiction remain **UNSET**.

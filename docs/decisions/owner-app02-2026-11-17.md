## Binding meaning and non-negotiable guardrails

- **P business fact:** a source-bound APP-01D OFFER, not cash transferred. It has a stable correlation identity and a committed non-posting `CashHandoverOffer`; production financial meaning is **not** created by the event wrapper.
- **C business fact:** a Cashier request to accept that particular OFFER. In APP-01D semantics an independently witnessed `CashierCountEvidence` is required; one accepted C atomically posts one `CashHandoverAcceptance`, exactly two custody/fund ledger legs, and **no second AR allocation**. An out-of-order C may be received before P without any acceptance effects. Two different event IDs must not bypass the **one successful acceptance per P** business uniqueness constraint.
- **Identity and authorization:** tenant is bound by server-side authenticated context, never by client-supplied tenant routing. Producer is a source system identity; ActorId comes from verified JWT at intake and is preserved. Revalidate current actor permissions for application/resume; revoked authorization is not resurrected by replay/restore. Do not reveal existence of another tenant's P.
- **Three different confirmations:** (1) intake ACK after receiver SQL COMMIT, (2) business `APPLIED` only after the acceptance and source/ledger COMMIT, (3) outbound ACK after the *consumer's* durable inbox COMMIT. No HTTP status alone proves a downstream business effect.
- **State boundaries:** `RECEIVED` → `PENDING_DEPENDENCY` while P is absent; `RECEIVED` or `PENDING_DEPENDENCY` → `APPLIED` only for a valid P and valid count/role; policy-invalid events may be reasoned `REJECTED` without business effects; timeout/retry exhaustion and unresolved exceptions become owned `CASE`. `CASE` may be resumed or closed only by recorded approval; `UNKNOWN` is lookup-side uncertainty, not a durable business state.
- **Retry and retention:** 15-minute scan is a safety net, not the sole delivery path. The six retry budget applies to *technical failures*, not dependency waiting. A 24-hour unresolved P creates CASE with `asOf`, owner, reason and next action. No silent expiry, no automatic history deletion; production retention policy remains open for APP-02B.

## 2. Binding authority and architecture boundaries

1. **Scope and mode:** Authorizes only nonproduction/PREPROD foundation work. Production A_ONLINE, B_ONLINE, Mixed, offline business writes, live cashier/invoice/accounting, pilot and go-live remain separately gated. `B_NO_CHANNEL` remains `BLOCKED`; production price/credit/terms and legal retention periods remain `UNSET` pending explicit approval. `SYNTHETIC_UNITLESS` and `invoiceEligible=false` still apply to historical synthetic paths.
2. **Isolation and identities:** One business seller per tenant, separate physical tenant database, authenticated server-bound tenant membership, current-role checks for API **and Worker**, no cross-tenant reads/search/exports/writes or metadata disclosure. Control Plane privileges do not inherit business-data access. The signed synthetic development/integration-test JWT, synthetic LocalDB credentials and synthetic endpoints must not be promoted to PREPROD/production privileges.
3. **Security lifecycle:** Login/refresh/rotation/revocation, epoch-based fences and out-of-band revocation truth must survive snapshot restores. Maker and Checker are distinct authorized actors as required by the business rule and cannot use retry/restore to bypass approvals; fail closed if membership/epoch/approval provenance cannot be established. Approvals and policy snapshots are immutable source-linked facts.
4. **Tenant lifecycle:** Administrative approval precedes provisioning; database creation, tenant binding and migrations must be retry-safe/idempotent and verify fresh/replay outcomes. Activation and reactivation require current health/isolation/auth checks. Suspension and decommission cannot silently delete or rewrite outstanding business facts; retain signed/audited ownership and next actions.
5. **Secrets and recovery:** Vault or equivalent must be protected behind a trust boundary distinct from application workloads. Signing-key versions, decryption-key availability, backup access and rotation/revocation must be auditable. A backup is not authority to reissue revoked credentials or resend external effects. Recovery must reconcile durable event/command/outbox/inbox outcomes and apply a fail-closed barrier for uncertainty.
6. **Audit and retention:** Separate security/business-evidence retention from short-lived diagnostic telemetry. Append-only/tamper-evident lineage and retention/archival legal holds require explicit owner/authority, including privacy constraints. **No assumed TTL purge** for durable APP-02A receipts, dependencies, CASE, ledger/source/outbox records.

## 4. Review-needed clarifications (NOT additional approved decisions)

The interview approved the **direction**, not every implementation detail. All six items are recorded as **`DEFERRED / BLOCKING`** in the [C.0 disposition table](../roadmap/app-02c0-a-online-e2e-contract.md#8-contract-review-dispositions-and-blocking-gates), with affected increments, closure evidence and approving roles. C-PLAN may accept those documented deferrals; it does not close them or authorize the blocked work. Do not silently invent values:

- **C0-R01 — Confirm vs BR-03 'giữ hàng':** Define exact intermediate Order state when `ConfirmAOrder` commits but `ReserveAStock` has not. Confirm must not be interpreted as an already committed reservation; UI and downstream rules must respect that distinction while preserving the business contract.
- **C0-R02 — Flexible ShipTo:** Freeze minimum address fields, region/country validation, access to one-off addresses, identity/provenance and amendment eligibility. Changes must produce a new version, never update source snapshots in place.
- **C0-R03 — Policy selection:** Freeze seller-local business date/timezone, validity/precedence/tie-break and snapshot hash/version; do not approve numbers implicitly.
- **C0-R04 — Partial logistics:** Confirm whether undelivered quantity may be `HELD` at trip and/or reservation layer, and what exact separately authorized close/release/unload sources clear it. Do not create silent release.
- **C0-R05 — Physical reviewer independence:** B.9 requires separate Security/DBA externally trusted reviewer keys and an independent observer. Prior solo-Owner exceptions for other PRs do not waive this. Design a legitimate external-review arrangement or obtain a separate, explicitly scoped Owner decision *before* claiming B.9 PASS; no false multi-person attestation.
- **C0-R06 — Acceptance matrix:** Approve source-specific expected fixtures, negative cases, and protocol *before* test execution. Do not predetermine pass counts or replace B.1 30 required physical cases.
